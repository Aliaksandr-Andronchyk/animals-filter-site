How to use Animals Filter

  • Tap Camera to photograph a pet, or Gallery to pick an existing photo.
  • The app recognizes the animal and shows a card with species, breed, approximate age, care tips and more.
  • For best results, use a clear, well-lit photo where the animal fills most of the frame.

Frequently asked

Why does it need camera and photo access? Only to capture or pick the picture you want to identify.

Does it work offline? Recognition is most accurate online. Without a connection the app uses a limited on-device fallback.

Which animals are supported? Common pets — dogs, cats, reptiles, birds, rodents, fish and more — including many breeds and morphs.
