Animals Filter

Point your camera at any pet and instantly learn what it is.

Animals Filter recognizes the animal in a photo and gives you a clean, readable card: species and breed, likely color morph, an approximate age estimate, origin, lifespan, temperament, habitat, diet and care tips — all in seconds.

🦎 Any pet. Dogs, cats, reptiles, birds, rodents, fish and more — including specific breeds and gecko morphs.
📷 Camera or gallery. Snap a new photo or pick an existing one.
⏳ Approximate age. A photo-based estimate of the animal's life stage, right at the top of the card.
🌍 Two languages. Works in English by default and in Russian on Russian devices.
